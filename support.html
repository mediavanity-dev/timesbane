The piece doesn't explain itself on purpose. If you would rather know, here is how to move around it.

Moving around

  • Begin: tap the breathing dot.
  • Just watch: each movement drifts into the next after a minute or two without touching.
  • Next movement: tap the slowly turning mark in the bottom-right corner, or swipe left with two fingers (right goes back).
  • Somewhere at random: press and hold the top-left corner until a ring closes, or turn the phone face down and back up.
  • Sound: the mark in the top-right corner turns it on and off. Sound plays even with the silent switch on.
  • Lenses: twist two fingers to see the movement through a different lens.
  • Everything else responds to touch, holding, dragging, double-tapping, tilting and shaking. Finding out how is the point.

Common questions

How do I add the widgets?

Touch and hold the Home Screen, tap + (or Edit → Add Widget), search for Art in Nature, and choose Today, Year, Moon or Tree. They also work in StandBy, while the iPhone charges on its side.

What does becoming a patron do?

Everything in the piece is free. The optional one-time Patron purchase supports it and adds two keepsakes: tap the screen with three fingers to keep a moment as a picture, and, while the phone is charging, the screen stays awake so the piece drifts on through the night. To see it, press and hold the turning mark in the bottom-right corner for about a second.

How do I restore the Patron purchase on a new device?

Press and hold the turning mark, then tap Restore it, signed in with the Apple Account you used.

Why does it ask for my location?

Only if you ask it to, from inside the piece: with it, the sky, Sun and Moon are drawn for exactly where you are. Without it, the piece guesses your city from your time zone. Your location stays on your device.

Is it the same as timesbane.com?

The same artwork. The app adds haptics, widgets, offline use and the patron's keepsakes; it leaves out a few live sources the website uses, such as local weather.
