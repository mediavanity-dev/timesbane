Art in Nature is a living artwork that doesn't explain itself. Touch it, tilt it, or simply watch: everything responds, everything sounds, and it is never the same twice.

Forty-eight movements drift into one another, each lasting a few minutes and unfolding slowly:
• A spiral galaxy, held together by nothing but a pattern in its orbits
• Ink dropped on water and combed into marbled paper
• Jellyfish glowing brighter as you sink into the deep
• A fractal coastline you can fall into for ever
• Curtains of aurora, a kelp forest, a meadow that blooms and goes to seed
• Fireflies that learn to flash as one, frost, rivers, dunes, lightning, a whole day passing over the hills
…and many more, some hidden, some only found by doing something.

LISTEN AND TOUCH
Every movement makes its own sound, made from what you see: orbits, ripples, falling sand, the length of a day's light. Haptics let you feel it too. Sound plays with the silent switch on.

IT KNOWS WHERE AND WHEN YOU ARE
The real Sun, Moon and stars, the hour, the season and the holidays where you live, and a few live signals from the planet: earthquakes, the Sun's weather, new Wikipedia pages as they are written.

NEVER THE SAME TWICE
New colours, shapes and keys every visit; lenses that change how you see a movement; secrets that leave gold stars in its night sky. It remembers you, and changes as you come back.

WIDGETS AND STANDBY
Today's pattern (the same one everyone on Earth is seeing this hour), the year as a spiral, tonight's Moon, and a tree that grows while you're not looking.

FREE, WHOLE, PRIVATE
No ads, no account, nothing locked. Everything it remembers stays on your device. An optional one-time Patron purchase supports it and adds two keepsakes: keep any moment as a picture, and let it drift on through the night while your phone charges.
