Privacy policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

The app (Art in Nature)

The app collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

What it remembers (your visits, the gestures it replays as your ghost, the things you have found) is kept on your device only, in the app's own files. It is never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your device (iCloud or a computer), it is included in that backup under your own account.

Location is used only if you ask for it from inside the piece, to draw the sky, Sun and Moon for where you are. It stays on your device and is never stored or sent.

Live data. To draw parts of the piece, the app fetches public information as it happens: space weather (NOAA), earthquakes (USGS), near-Earth objects (NASA), new Wikipedia pages (Wikimedia), the position of the International Space Station (wheretheiss.at), new Bitcoin blocks (mempool.space), public holidays (Nager.Date, using the country from your time zone), Arctic sea ice (global-warming.org), a painting's colour (the Art Institute of Chicago) and, in Great Britain, the electricity grid's carbon intensity. These requests carry no personal information; like any web request they reach those services from your device's internet address. Nothing is kept by us.

Movement and sound. Tilt, shake and the battery level are read on the device to shape the piece and never leave it.

Keeping a moment creates a picture on your device; it goes only where you choose through the share sheet.

Purchases. The optional Patron purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

The website (timesbane.com)

The website shows the same artwork in your browser and keeps its memory in your browser's storage. It uses Google Analytics to count ordinary page visits. If you choose to share your location on the website, your place, rounded to about 10 km, is sent to Open-Meteo (weather, air, sea, elevation and past weather) and iNaturalist (species counts near you); exact coordinates are never stored or sent.

Email

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
